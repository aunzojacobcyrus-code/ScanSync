{% icon "info" 14 %} Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Are raw photos stored on external servers?

No. ScanSync computes mathematical face vectors (128 floating-point numbers) inside your browser. Matching is done against your local database without external third-party cloud AI vendors.

What happens if a student arrives after the grace period?

If a class starts at 8:00 AM with a 15-minute grace tolerance, arrival before 8:15 AM is tagged Present. Arrival after 8:15 AM is automatically recorded as Late with the exact arrival timestamp.

Can students scan twice for the same class meeting?

No. ScanSync has built-in duplicate-scan protection. Once a student is recognized for a session, their record is locked and subsequent scans during that class are safely ignored.

Does ScanSync work offline in the local network?

Yes. The face detection neural model weights and all icons/scripts are cached locally, allowing ScanSync to operate inside local campus LANs without constant internet connection.
